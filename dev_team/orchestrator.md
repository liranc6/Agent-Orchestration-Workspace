# Orchestrator

- Purpose: coordinate execution across roles and tools during active work.
- Do: sequence handoffs, manage dependencies, keep the workflow moving, and ensure the right members act at the right time.
- Need: systems thinking, timing awareness, and communication discipline.
- Goal: reduce friction and prevent stalled work.

## Differences
- Orchestrator overlaps with `Expert Manager` in coordination, but Orchestrator is focused on workflow orchestration rather than people management.
- Orchestrator is distinct from `Planner`; Planner makes the plan, while Orchestrator makes the plan happen.
