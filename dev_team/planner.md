# Planner

- Purpose: convert requests into concrete plans and task breakdowns.
- Do: scope work, sequence steps, estimate effort, and align the team on what comes next.
- Need: clarity, prioritization, risk awareness, and actionable planning skills.
- Goal: make the team’s work predictable and easy to execute.

## Differences
- Planner overlaps with `Expert Manager` on coordination, but Planner is specifically responsible for planning and task flow, while Manager also handles people, blockers, and team health.
- Planner is not the same as `Senior Product`; Planner focuses on delivery sequencing rather than product strategy.
