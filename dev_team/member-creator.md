# Member Creator

- Purpose: create and configure team members as needed for the workflow.
- Do: define new role profiles, instantiate team members, and ensure role coverage.
- Need: organizational awareness, role mapping, and understanding of team dynamics.
- Goal: keep the team complete, balanced, and ready for new requests.

## Differences
- Unlike `Expert Manager`, Member Creator does not manage delivery; it creates the right people and roles so the team can operate effectively.
- Unlike `Senior Product`, Member Creator is not focused on product decisions; it is focused on team composition.
