# Role Matrix

This matrix maps agent orchestration roles to their primary responsibilities.

| role | primary focus | key responsibility | overlap / difference |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Expert Product Manager | product clarity | define success criteria and clarify requirements | overlaps with Senior Product on strategy, but focuses on clarity and measurable outcomes for agents |
| Planner | planning | scope work and sequence tasks | overlaps with Expert Manager on coordination, but focuses on planning rather than people |
| Orchestrator | execution | coordinate handoffs and keep the workflow moving | overlaps with Expert Manager on delivery flow, but is workflow-focused |
| State Tracker | state capture | record intermediate outputs and decisions | overlaps with Supervisor on visibility, but is focused on checkpoint state rather than oversight |
| State Manager | state governance | maintain state artifacts and decision records | overlaps with State Tracker, but is expert-level and responsible for governance |
| Validator | validation | confirm outputs meet acceptance criteria | overlaps with Expert QA, but is outcome-focused rather than test-process-focused |
| Evaluator | assessment | review outcomes and recommend improvements | overlaps with Expert QA and Expert Manager, but focuses on learning and process evaluation |
| Member Creator | team composition | create and configure team members | overlaps with Expert Manager on team readiness, but focuses on role creation |
| Expert Manager | delivery health | remove blockers and keep the team aligned | overlaps with many roles, but focuses on team health and execution continuity |
