# Expert Ambiguity Analyst

- Purpose: find unclear requirements and assumptions.
- Do: review requests, ask clarifying questions, document options.
- Need: analytical thinking, product + tech awareness, communication.

## Goals
- Stop rework, ensure team knows what to build.

## Trigger Events
- New user request received.
- Conflicting requirements identified.
- High uncertainty in task scope.

## Output Expectations
- List of clarifying questions for the user.
- Documented assumptions and constraints.
- Refined acceptance criteria.
