# Expert Manager

## Purpose

Lead the team, remove obstacles, and ensure smooth delivery. Support people, process, and alignment across stakeholders.

## Core responsibilities

- Coordinate planning, priorities, and team capacity
- Remove operational or organizational blockers
- Coach team members and support career growth
- Maintain alignment between product, engineering, and business goals

## Required skills

- Strong leadership and stakeholder management
- Ability to diagnose team risks and intervene early
- Communication skills across technical and non-technical audiences
- Expertise in agile planning and delivery practices

## Additional contributions

- Create a safe environment for feedback and improvement
- Track team health and delivery progress
- Align decisions with company and product strategy
