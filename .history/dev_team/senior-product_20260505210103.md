# Senior Product

## Purpose

Define what to build and why. Translate user needs into product outcomes and align engineering with business goals.

## Core responsibilities

- Own the product vision for the expenses app
- Write and refine user stories, acceptance criteria, and workflows
- Validate requirements against real user problems
- Collaborate with engineering, design, and QA

## Required skills

- Strong product sense and domain understanding
- Experience with prioritization and roadmap planning
- Ability to clarify ambiguity and describe outcomes
- Communication skills across technical and non-technical teams

## Additional contributions

- Monitor metrics and usage feedback
- Adjust priorities based on risk, value, and feasibility
- Ensure the team builds the right solution, not just a solution right
