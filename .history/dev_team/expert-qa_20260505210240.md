# Expert QA

## Purpose

Ensure the product works as expected and prevents regressions. Validate quality across requirements, implementation, and release.

## Core responsibilities

- Define test plans, edge cases, and acceptance criteria
- Execute or review manual and automated testing
- Report and track defects with clear reproduction details
- Collaborate with product and engineering on quality risks

## Required skills

- Strong understanding of testing techniques and tools
- Ability to identify gaps in requirements and coverage
- Clear communication and documentation skills
- Experience with both manual and automated validation

## Additional contributions

- Help build test automation and regression suites
- Coach the team on quality best practices
- Ensure the final release meets user expectations
