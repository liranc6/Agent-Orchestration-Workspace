# Senior / Expert / Principal Dev Programmer

## Purpose

Lead engineering delivery, design durable solutions, and mentor the team. Own technical strategy and ensure implementation quality.

## Core responsibilities

- Architect and review complex features
- Define system design, APIs, and data models
- Mentor junior developers and raise team capability
- Ensure maintainability, performance, and observability

## Required skills

- Deep experience in the project stack and architecture
- Strong problem-solving and design skills
- Excellent code review and refactoring ability
- Ability to balance pragmatism with long-term quality

## Additional contributions

- Drive technical roadmap and platform decisions
- Help remove blockers and align engineering with product goals
- Advocate for automation, testability, and reliability
