# Expert DevOps / Infrastructure Engineer

## Purpose

Build and maintain reliable deployment, CI/CD, and infrastructure automation. Ensure the app runs smoothly in development and production.

## Core responsibilities

- Design and operate build, test, and deployment pipelines
- Manage environments, configuration, and observability
- Ensure reliability, security, and operational readiness
- Support infrastructure-related incidents and recovery

## Required skills

- Experience with CI/CD, deployments, and cloud infrastructure
- Knowledge of observability, logging, and monitoring practices
- Strong automation and scripting ability
- Understanding of security, backup, and release processes

## Additional contributions

- Enable fast, safe releases and development feedback loops
- Reduce manual operational work through automation
- Support disaster recovery and post-deployment validation
