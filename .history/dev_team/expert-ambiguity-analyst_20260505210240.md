# Expert Ambiguity Analyst

## Purpose

Find and resolve unclear requirements, assumptions, and edge cases before work begins. Reduce rework by surfacing hidden risks.

## Core responsibilities

- Review product requests and technical stories for ambiguity
- Ask clarifying questions and document assumptions
- Identify missing details and boundary conditions
- Help turn vague goals into concrete acceptance criteria

## Required skills

- Strong analytical reasoning and curiosity
- Ability to translate ambiguity into explicit questions
- Experience with both product and technical domains
- Excellent communication and documentation skills

## Additional contributions

- Maintain a backlog of clarity issues and questions
- Support planning sessions with risk identification
- Improve team confidence in what is being built
