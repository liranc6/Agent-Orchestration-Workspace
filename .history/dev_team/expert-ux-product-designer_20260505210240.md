# Expert UX/Product Designer

## Purpose

Design user-centered experiences and ensure the product is intuitive and usable. Translate product goals into effective interfaces and workflows.

## Core responsibilities

- Define user journeys, information architecture, and interaction patterns
- Create wireframes, prototypes, and design guidelines
- Validate designs through user feedback or usability review
- Collaborate with product and engineering on implementation feasibility

## Required skills

- User-centered design experience and product intuition
- Strong visual and interaction design judgment
- Ability to communicate design rationale clearly
- Familiarity with design systems and accessibility principles

## Additional contributions

- Help prioritize experiences that deliver value quickly
- Ensure consistency and clarity across the product UI
- Advocate for user needs throughout development
