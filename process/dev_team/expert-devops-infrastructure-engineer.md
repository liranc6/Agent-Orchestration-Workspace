- Purpose: keep deployment and infra reliable.
- Do: build CI/CD, manage envs, monitor, automate ops.

- Fast safe releases, stable runtime, low ops friction.

- Environment setup or configuration changes.

## Output Expectations
- Infrastructure-as-code snippets or scripts.
- Deployment strategies and environment validations.
- Operational checklists.
